How this tool works

A short, plain account of what is behind the result.

The data
The public Student Depression dataset from Kaggle. It had 27,901 rows, which became 27,828 students after cleaning.
The model
A Random Forest classifier that looks at academic pressure, financial stress, study satisfaction, work and study hours, sleep, eating habits, age, CGPA, gender and family history. It scored about 79% accuracy on students it had not seen before.
What it leaves out
The dataset also records suicidal thoughts. This site does not ask about that, because a website is the wrong place for it. If that is on your mind, please use the help numbers below.
Limits
The answers in the dataset were self-reported, patterns are not causes, and the model will be wrong for some people. Treat the result as a prompt to reflect, not a verdict.

If things feel heavy, talk to someone

You do not need to wait until it feels serious. These services are free to call in India. Please check each number on its official site before relying on it.

Tele-MANAS 14416

Government of India mental health helpline, open all day and night. Also reachable on 1-800-891-4416.

KIRAN 1800-599-0019

Government of India mental health rehabilitation helpline.

Emergency 112

If you or someone near you may be in immediate danger, call this number now. You can also talk to your college counsellor or someone you trust.
